# My-cool-game
A fun 2D game. Collect coins, stomp slimes bounce off mushrooms and reach the end of the world. But most importantly, don't fall down.

How to play: Move Left with the left arrow key or the a key. Move right with the right arrow key or the d key. jump with space. You can press escape or press the pause button at the top right to pause the game. 

Reach the flag at the end of the level to progress to the next level. At the end of level 2 there is a sign and interact with it using the e key and you can experience an epic end scene.

Features: 

Slime stomping. Slimes die after jumping on their head three times.

Mushroom bouncing. If you see a red and white mushroom, you can jump on its head to bounce.

Three hearts per run. Each run you get three lives and before you start you have the option to turn on checkpoints. Turning on checkpoints allows you to respawn where you died and not at the start of the level.

High score. The amount of coins collected is saved per run and you can reset it if you want.

Pause menu. Press escape or press the pause button at the top right of your screen to pause your game.

End screen. Reach the end and see a scrolling end screen with your lifetime stats of coins picked up, slimes defeated, times died and time spent in the game.
